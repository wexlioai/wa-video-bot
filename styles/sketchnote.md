# Style: Sketchnote

A smart person's notebook coming alive: marker lines draw themselves, doodles and arrows connect ideas,
highlighter swipes mark what matters. Feels human, clear and friendly.

## Look
- Off-white paper (`bg`), optional faint dot grid. Lines are hand-drawn: SVG paths with slight wobble, round caps, 5-9 px stroke in `ink`.
- Doodle icons (lightbulb, clock, house, person, coin, heart, arrows, stars, speech bubbles), simple boxes and banners, underlines and circles around key words.
- Colour comes from 1-2 marker/highlighter colours (`primary`, `accent`) used as fills behind words or shapes at 40-70 % opacity.

## Palette rules
- `bg` warm white or light paper; `bg2` slightly darker paper; `ink` near-black marker; `primary` and `accent` highlighter-like but not fluorescent (marigold, coral, teal, lilac).

## Type
- Hand lettering: Caveat, Kalam or Patrick Hand for headlines and notes; a clean sans (Nunito, Poppins) for numbers if needed.
- Urdu: Noto Nastaliq Urdu.

## Composition
- Ideas spread across the page like notes, connected by arrows; each scene adds one cluster. Lots of white space.

## Motion vocabulary
- Line drawing with stroke-dashoffset (the signature move) synced to the words; text writes on letter by letter (clip-path reveal left→right).
- Highlighter swipe (scaleX from 0) behind key words; circles drawn around numbers.
- Page slides or a camera pan to the next cluster. Small wiggle on doodles (rotation ±3°).

## Don't
- No photos, glossy gradients, 3D, neon, glitch, code, HUDs, game words.
