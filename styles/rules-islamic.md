# Rules for Islamic scholarship and history videos (follow strictly)

These videos are about Islamic law, the Prophet's life (sira), history, hadith, ethics and scholarship.
The audience expects authority, accuracy and reverence.

## Never
- Never depict the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, any other prophet, his family, the Companions or angels in any form:
  no faces, heads, silhouettes, hands, shadows, figures, stick figures or characters, not even stylised,
  blurred or seen from behind. If a scene is about them, show places, documents, objects, light, patterns,
  text, maps or symbols instead.
- No people at all by default. Groups/communities are shown as banners, shields, coloured markers, dots on a
  map, tents or houses, or names in text. (Simple anonymous crowds as tiny dots on a map are fine.)
- Never write Qur'an verses or hadith in Arabic yourself. Show the reference (e.g. "Qur'an 49:13", "Sahih
  al-Bukhari 7138") and, if the narration quotes a translation, the English/Urdu words from the narration.
  Arabic text may appear only when it is printed in the script (for example a case's Arabic title).
- Never invent facts, dates, numbers, names or sources. Use only what the narration and plan say.
- Nothing playful or casual: no cartoon characters, mascots, emoji, jokes, bouncy squash-and-stretch, game
  words, neon, glitch, cyberpunk or sci-fi visuals.
- No images of the Kaaba's interior, no imagined depictions of the Prophet's grave or of sacred relics.

## Always
- When the Prophet is named on screen, write ﷺ after the name (exactly this character). Keep other honorifics
  as the script writes them.
- Dates as the text gives them, ideally both: "1 AH / 622 CE".
- Motion is calm and precise: ink lines drawing, documents settling, slow camera moves, light shifting,
  patterns building. Durations generous (0.8-1.4 s), easing power2/power3/sine. One strong accent moment per
  scene at most (a seal, a stamp, a reveal).
- Typography is classical and well set: serif headlines, generous spacing, nothing cramped.
- Sacred names and terms are treated with care: never distorted, never animated with shakes or bounces,
  never placed on the floor of a scene, under feet or in a trash/negative context.
- Architecture and geometry (arches, domes, muqarnas, girih stars, lattice light) are welcome as visual
  language; use them respectfully and accurately (the 8-point star, 6-fold and 10-fold patterns).
