# Style: Historical Map Documentary

A cinematic history documentary told with maps and timelines: the land, the cities, the routes and the
dates. For sira (without any figures), history, campaigns, migrations and the spread of ideas.

## Look
- Stylised maps drawn with SVG: land in warm sand tones, sea in muted blue, subtle relief hatching, a thin
  coastline; cities as small circles with serif labels; regions as soft tinted areas with dashed borders.
- Approximate geography of the Arabian Peninsula and neighbouring regions: Mecca and Medina in the west
  (Hijaz) along the Red Sea, Medina about 340 km north of Mecca; Ta'if east of Mecca; Badr south-west of
  Medina near the coast; Khaybar north of Medina; Jerusalem, Damascus north; Kufa, Basra, Baghdad in Iraq
  to the north-east; Yemen in the south. Keep it schematic, not a precise atlas, and label everything.
- Communities as banners or shields with names; movement as dashed routes with an arrow head; areas of
  influence as soft tinted circles.
- A timeline ruler along the bottom or side with AH and CE marks; the current date highlighted.
- Insets: a circle zoom on a city, a parchment card with the key fact.

## Palette rules
- `bg` warm sand or parchment, `bg2` muted sea blue-grey, `ink` dark brown, `primary` deep green or lapis
  for highlights, `accent` crimson or gold for routes and the current focus.

## Type
- Serif headlines (Lora, Libre Baskerville), labels in Inter 600 small caps.

## Motion
- Camera flies over the map (scale and translate of the map group) to the place being discussed.
- Routes draw along their path; cities pop in with a ring pulse (one pulse, not looping).
- Timeline marker slides to the date; years count.
- Banners rise from the map at their location.
