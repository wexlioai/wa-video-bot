# Retention: design so nobody wants to skip

Viewers decide in the first 3 seconds and keep deciding every 5-10 seconds. Every scene must earn the next
one. These rules apply in every style (and never override the niche rules: dignity first, no cheap tricks).

## The first 3 seconds (hook scene and first scene)
- Frame 0 is already a strong, finished-looking image: no empty screen, no slow fade from black, no logo.
- The hook's question or surprising fact appears big and fast (within 0.4 s), with one striking visual
  (a seal slamming, a map zooming to the place, a pattern building, a date counting back 1,400 years).
- Motion starts immediately and keeps going.

## Never a dead moment
- Something meaningful changes on screen at least every 2-4 seconds: a new element arrives with the word,
  a highlight sweeps, the camera moves to the next detail, a number counts, a line draws.
- No static hold longer than 4 seconds. During long sentences, slowly move the camera across the composition
  or reveal supporting details one by one.
- Build up, don't dump: a scene starts simple and ends rich. Never show the whole layout at once.

## Curiosity and structure
- Section openers announce what's coming in 1-1.5 s (kicker "THE FACTS", "THE RULING", a number) so viewers
  feel progress.
- Pose before you answer: show the question (the issue, the dilemma) clearly before the answer arrives;
  for a case, keep the ruling as the payoff: build suspense visually before the holding (a sealed document,
  the court's name, then the stamp).
- Emphasise the 1-3 words that matter in each sentence exactly when they are spoken (colour, underline,
  scale). Numbers and dates always count or tick into place.
- Vary rhythm: alternate wide shots (map, full document) and close-ups (one word, one clause, one seal).

## The ending
- The last scene lands on a strong, simple final image with the key takeaway in a few words.
- Leave the right half or the bottom-right of the final 8 seconds calm (end-screen buttons go there).

## Layout reserved by the bot
- The top 100 px (long) or top 240 px (Short) hold the progress line and section names. Keep important
  things out of them.
