# Style: Calligraphic Kinetic

Words as the visual: a key term in large, beautiful type with its meaning unfolding around it, lots of
space and calm rhythm. For concepts, definitions, quotations and Shorts.

## Look
- One key term per scene, very large (180-300 px). If the script contains the Arabic term (e.g. شورى), show
  it as printed in Amiri or Reem Kufi, with the transliteration (Shura) and the meaning in smaller type.
- Thin geometric ornaments (a single star, a line, a frame corner) support the type.
- Plain deep or parchment backgrounds with gentle light.

## Palette rules
- `bg` deep green, ink black-blue or parchment; `ink` maximum contrast; `primary` and `accent` gold and one
  calm colour.

## Type
- Arabic only from the script (Amiri, Reem Kufi, Scheherazade New); Latin in Cormorant Garamond or Cinzel;
  labels Inter.

## Motion
- The term writes on (clip-path reveal in reading direction, right-to-left for Arabic), then the meaning
  fades up line by line exactly with the voice.
- Slow scale 1 → 1.04 during holds; a single ornament draws around the term.
