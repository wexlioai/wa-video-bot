# Style: Soft Clay 3D

Friendly, rounded objects that look like soft clay or matte plastic: chunky shapes, gentle shading,
bouncy motion. Warm and approachable.

## Look
- Fake 3D with CSS/SVG: rounded shapes with a radial-gradient highlight (top-left) and a soft darker edge (bottom-right), soft ambient shadow underneath. Matte, never glossy or chrome.
- Chunky, simplified objects (pills, blobs, cups, plants, phones, hearts, coins, characters with bean bodies).
- Soft pastel or warm backgrounds with a floor plane (subtle gradient) and contact shadows.

## Palette rules
- `bg` soft pastel or warm neutral; `bg2` a slightly deeper tone for the floor; `ink` deep, warm dark; `primary` main clay colour; `accent` cheerful contrasting clay colour. Mid saturation, no neon.

## Type
- Rounded or friendly sans: Nunito, Fredoka, Baloo 2, Quicksand (700-900). Text can sit on a clay pill.

## Composition
- One hero object big and centred or on a third, 2-3 small props, lots of air.

## Motion vocabulary
- Squash and stretch: scaleY/scaleX overshoot on landing (`elastic.out(1, 0.5)` or `back.out(2)`), jelly wobble.
- Objects pop, bounce, roll; gentle idle bob (y ±10 px). Transitions: a big clay shape rolls across the screen.

## Don't
- No metallic or glass looks, neon, glitch, code, HUDs, game words.
