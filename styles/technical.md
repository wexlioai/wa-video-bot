# Style: Technical Drawing

Precise line drawings that construct themselves on light paper: blueprints-in-reverse (dark lines on
light), callouts, dimension lines and exploded views. Clear, intelligent, calm.

## Look
- Light paper or light grey-blue `bg` with a faint engineering grid. Thin precise strokes (2-4 px) in `ink`; one `accent` for the highlighted part.
- Objects drawn as outlines with hidden lines dashed, dimension arrows with small numbers, callout circles with leader lines and labels.
- Exploded views: parts separated along an axis to show how something is built.

## Palette rules
- `bg` paper white, light grey or very pale blue (NOT dark blueprint navy); `bg2` slightly darker paper; `ink` graphite or deep navy; `primary` a muted engineering blue or green for fills; `accent` warm orange or red for the key part.

## Type
- Clean sans: Inter, Manrope, Archivo (500-700); labels in small caps with letter-spacing. Avoid monospace.

## Composition
- One object centred or at a third, callouts radiating out; a title block in a corner like a drawing sheet.

## Motion vocabulary
- Lines draw themselves (stroke-dashoffset), in construction order; fills fade in after outlines.
- Parts slide apart (exploded) and back together; callouts pop with their leader lines drawing.
- Slow rotation is faked by switching drawings, not 3D; camera zoom into a detail.

## Don't
- No dark sci-fi blueprints, glowing lines, HUDs, code, terminals, neon, game words.
