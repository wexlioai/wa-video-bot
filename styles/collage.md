# Style: Documentary Collage

A storyteller's collage: framed photo-like shapes, torn paper, tape, map lines and handwritten notes,
layered on textured paper. For true stories, travel and history.

## Look
- "Photos" are not real images: draw them as framed rectangles filled with duotone illustrated silhouettes (landscape, building, person, object) in palette colours, with a white border and a slight tilt.
- Torn-paper edges, masking tape strips, paper clips, stamps, map fragments with dotted routes and pins, date labels.
- Textured paper background; layered depth with soft shadows.

## Palette rules
- `bg` warm paper; `bg2` darker paper/cardboard; `ink` dark brown or charcoal; `primary` an earthy hue (terracotta, olive, slate); `accent` a marker red or ochre for routes and highlights.

## Type
- Serif headlines (Libre Baskerville, Lora, Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond); handwritten notes (Caveat, Kalam); dates in small caps sans.

## Composition
- A pinboard spread: 1 main framed piece, 2-3 supporting scraps, a note or label. Each scene adds pieces to the board or pans across it.

## Motion vocabulary
- Pieces slide in and settle with a small rotation; tape appears last (scale from 0). Routes draw on maps (stroke-dashoffset), pins drop.
- Slow camera pans across a large board (translate the whole layer), gentle push-in (scale 1 → 1.06).

## Don't
- No real photos of real people, no neon, glitch, code, HUDs, game words.
