# Style: Kinetic Type Minimal

The words are the visuals: big, beautiful type moving with the voice in lots of empty space. Ideal for
quotes, motivation and Shorts.

## Look
- Large type (120-260 px) in 1-2 families, strong weight contrast. One idea per frame, 1-6 words on screen.
- Minimal shapes only as support: a line, a dot, a single circle or bar in `accent`.
- Plain or softly two-tone backgrounds; colour blocks that change between beats.

## Palette rules
- `bg` either very light or a confident mid/dark colour (not black-and-neon); `bg2` the alternate block colour; `ink` maximum contrast on bg; `primary` for alternate words; `accent` for the key word.

## Type
- Display: Archivo Black, Bebas Neue, Playfair Display, Fraunces, DM Serif Display; supporting: Inter, Manrope.
- Urdu: Gulzar large, Noto Nastaliq Urdu.

## Composition
- Text placed with intention (left-aligned blocks, stacked lines, one huge word). Respect safe areas; Shorts use the middle third.

## Motion vocabulary
- Words appear exactly on their spoken time (`when('word')`), masked rises, slides, scale punches (1.15 → 1), letter-by-letter for emphasis.
- Background colour blocks wipe between beats; the key word shifts colour or gets an underline sweep.
- Keep rhythm: hold, move, hold. Never jitter.

## Don't
- No glitch text, RGB split, neon glow, typewriter/terminal effects, monospace, game words.
